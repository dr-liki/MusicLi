MusicLi is an independent FOSS Android music player built with Jetpack Compose, Material 3 Expressive, and AndroidX Media3, focused on local playback, self-hosted libraries, synced lyrics, and user-controlled online features.

The app works 100% offline by default. Optional online services stay off unless enabled or triggered by you.

Features:

* Local music scanning and playback with Media3 + FFmpeg (MP3, FLAC, AAC, OGG, WAV, M4A)
* Gapless playback, crossfade, custom transitions, queue controls, shuffle, repeat, sleep timer, and 0.25x–3x playback speed
* Albums, artists, genres, folders, favorites, playlists, stats, metadata editing, and Storage Sense cache manager
* Synced & plain lyrics across 9 providers with priority-ordered bulk auto-embedding, live translation, and Urdu / Indic romanization
* Synced YouTube cover-video playback with fullscreen lock, quality persistence, and SponsorBlock offsets
* Optional Explore discovery, streaming, and persistent offline M4A + LRC downloads
* Self-hosted Navidrome/Subsonic and Jellyfin sync, streaming, and app-private offline downloads
* Material 3 Expressive UI, dynamic color, AMOLED/light/dark themes, Discover Carousel, Glance widgets, and Android Auto
* Full .pxpl backup/restore and .m3u8 sync compatibility with PixelPlayerOSS
* GitHub Releases-only updates across Stable and Alpha channels

MusicLi contains no ads, analytics, telemetry, Firebase, Crashlytics, Play Store billing, or Google Play Services runtime dependencies.
